## Overview
*Hereditary* is a 2018 psychological horror film directed by **Ari Aster**.  
It follows a family haunted by a mysterious and terrifying presence after the death of their grandmother.  
The film explores themes of **grief, trauma, and fate**, blending emotional storytelling with supernatural horror.
